About me

(01)Who I am

Marketer by trade, curious by nature. Outside work I dive, volunteer, model now and then, and read about money and technology.

I grew up in the Philippines, worked in the UAE, and now live in Kyoto. Most of what I know about people I learned outside an office: on youth councils, in live-production booths, and underwater.

You don't need everything to be perfect to start. You just need to start.

(02)Finance & web3

I've been interested in finance since university, where I was a member of the UP Junior Finance Association (2018–2019). Lately that interest has turned to web3 and how money works on the blockchain.

At an AI Summer School hackathon, our team built spending limits for AI agents on the blockchain, a way to let AI agents pay for things without handing them an open wallet.

Hackathon · AI Summer SchoolWe built spending limits for AI agents

(03)Modeling

I also work as a freelance model. Being on the other side of the camera taught me a lot about how brands are photographed, and how much a good shoot depends on everyone feeling comfortable.

(04)The ocean

I'm a scuba diving instructor and have transplanted coral reefs in Anilao since 2015. Diving taught me underwater photography and videography, a lot about marine life, and a more eco-friendly way of living.

(05)UP Bike Share

One of the nicest experiences I've ever had.

I was a founding member of UP Bike Share at the University of the Philippines, from 2015 to 2021: a student-run bike-sharing group on campus.

Along the way I volunteered at our events, including Sabay Lakbay, an eco-tour on bikes, and the Lakbay2Love movie premiere, where I handled food and drinks for the production crew and volunteers. Building something from nothing with friends, and watching people actually use it, is a feeling I still chase in my work.

(06)Live production

For years I volunteered in live production: multimedia at Destiny Church Manila (2011–2014), then graphics, audio, video and lighting at Christ's Commission Fellowship (2015–2019). It's where I learned to stay calm when something goes wrong live, and to trust a team completely.
