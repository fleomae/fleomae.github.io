Case study 06

MAD Marketing Studio

RoleSocial Media Director
WhenDec 2021 – Aug 2023
ChannelsTikTok, Instagram, Facebook, X, YouTube, web
WherePhilippines · clients in Spain, South Korea, UK

(01)The challenge

Around ten international clients from Spain, South Korea and the UK, across cosmetics, skincare, food and beverages, bags, tumblers and pet food. Each one had its own audience and goals, so no two plans looked the same.

(02)What I did

  • Planned and ran the marketing for each client, including social content for Korean skincare, beauty and lifestyle brands.
  • Ran KOL and influencer campaigns that grew followers and generated user-generated content.
  • Used social listening to hear what communities were saying, and proposed brand improvements based on it.
  • Managed website content, and aligned messaging with each client's marketing, operations and leadership teams.
  • Monitored performance and adapted each strategy based on the data.

(03)What happened

72%
Organic follower growth in a year
3×
F&B client sales
Sold out
Client events, fully booked

A salon client beat its pre-pandemic sales record, and one F&B client was so happy with the results that they invited me to host their celebration.

(04)What I took away

Marketing is about connection: understanding what excites people, what makes them click, and what turns them into loyal customers. Listening comes first.

Fun fact

I enjoyed working alongside a team of very creative people.
