Case study 07

L'Oréal Garnier

RoleE-Commerce Brand Activation Manager
WhenJun 2021 – Dec 2021
ChannelsShopee, Lazada, Zalora, TikTok
WherePhilippines · via Great Deals E-Commerce Corp.

(01)The challenge

Garnier was new to the market and had to stand out in a very competitive skincare category, especially during the big peak shopping events. I joined the account because I love skincare.

(02)What I did

  • Led e-commerce strategy and promotions across Shopee, Lazada and Zalora.
  • Analyzed consumer behavior and trends to improve product listings, pricing and promotion bundles.
  • Managed execution end to end: page optimization, creative direction, banners and ad integration.
  • Pushed for early adoption of TikTok commerce, working with influencers to show the products in use.
  • Learned L'Oréal's brand values and distribution strategy under the direct guidance of the former head of e-commerce.

(03)What happened

#2
Skincare category ranking on 11.11
PHP 246M
Sales, approx. USD 4.4M

Our marketplace stores grew, and my market research fed into the brand's long-term strategy. Garnier moved into TikTok commerce early, and it still sells there today.

(04)What I took away

E-commerce is about spotting trends and acting at the right moment. Wins don't happen overnight, and every number, promo and strategy has a purpose.

Fun fact

Working at a large corporation changed how I see business: clear goals, smart strategy, and everyone working toward the same target.
