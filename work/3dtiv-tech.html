Case study 04

3DTIV TECH

RoleMarketing Head · Punch Limited
WhenDec 2023 – Mar 2026
ChannelsWebsite, LinkedIn, Instagram, brochures, Google Ads, Expo events
WhereDubai, UAE · serving the GCC and East Africa

(01)The challenge

3D printing is not an easy product to market.

The machines were expensive, the technology was technical, and we were talking to engineers and procurement teams in industries where people expect you to know what you're talking about. In the Gulf, most manufacturers still worked the traditional, subtractive way.

I couldn't market the product properly without understanding it first.

(02)What I did

I started by learning the technology properly: how the metal and polymer printing systems worked, what materials they used, and, more importantly, what engineers actually cared about.

From there, I worked on the messaging, website, brochures, LinkedIn, Instagram, Google Ads and event materials. The details, for the curious:

  • The technology itself: laser powder-bed fusion for metals (316L stainless steel, AlSi10Mg aluminum, CuSn10 bronze, Inconel 718, Ti6Al4V titanium) and for polymers (PA11, PA12, TPU and carbon-fiber-filled powders).
  • Met and presented to people from defense, nuclear energy, oil & gas, aerospace and automotive, and shaped the messaging around what each of them needed.
  • Turned machine specs into things people could picture: faster prototypes, lighter and more complex parts, and fewer delays waiting for spare parts.
  • Made the product brochures (like the FS200M), website content, social posts, Google Ads and Expo materials.
