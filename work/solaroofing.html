Case study 05

Solaroofing

RoleMarketing Head · Punch Limited
WhenDec 2023 – Mar 2026
ChannelsWebsite, solar calculator, Instagram, LinkedIn
WhereDubai, UAE

(01)The challenge

A roofing, waterproofing and solar PV company with more than 30 years in Dubai, serving homes, businesses and government clients. Its website didn't explain the solar side clearly, and homeowners had no simple way to see what solar could save them.

(02)What I did

  • Designed the new Solaroofing website, with a clear structure for its three services: roof repairs, waterproofing and solar PV systems.
  • Helped finish the website's solar energy calculator, working with our intern, an engineer colleague and my director, so visitors can estimate what solar could do for their building.
  • Wrote the website and social content, turning technical roofing and solar details into plain language for homeowners and facility managers.
  • Ran Solaroofing's social media as part of the wider Punch Limited strategy.

(03)What happened

Solaroofing got a website that explains all three services clearly, plus a working solar calculator visitors can try before they get in touch. The Solaroofing team also hit its goal of reducing project downtime during my time at Punch.
