Case study 02

MakerSpace Delft

RoleWebsite Designer & Social Media Manager
WhenApr 2025 – Dec 2025
ChannelsWebflow, Figma, Instagram
WhereNetherlands (remote)

(01)The challenge

MakerSpace Delft gives Delft's technical and creative students access to tools, knowledge and community. Its website wasn't mobile-friendly, and its social channels didn't reflect how much was happening in the space.

(02)What I did

  • Redesigned the website in Figma and rebuilt it in Webflow with a small team, including James Murdza and Prajesh Kalikadien, improving user experience and mobile responsiveness.
  • Created a consistent brand presence across social media, especially Instagram.
  • Made short-form videos and carousel posts to promote workshops, events and community initiatives.
  • Planned a monthly social calendar around MakerSpace events and goals, and worked with partners on community stories and innovation-focused campaigns.
  • Tracked performance and reported insights to improve reach and engagement.

(03)What happened

A clean, mobile-friendly site that improved engagement, and one brand voice from the website to Instagram. The MakerSpace team gave us good feedback on the result.

(04)What I took away

Working with a distributed team across time zones, with design, development and content all moving at once, showed me how much good tools and clear communication matter.

Fun fact

I got to work on this with friends from the Netherlands. It's rare to combine professional growth with such good company!
