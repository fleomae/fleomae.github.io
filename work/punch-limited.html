Punch Limited

RoleMarketing Head
WhenDec 2023 – Mar 2026
ChannelsInstagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, X, Meta & Google Ads, email, Expo events
WhereUAE
Modest Company · brand filmOur Journey: From Prefab Beginnings to a Growing Family of Businesses

(01)The challenge

Five very different businesses (Modest Company, Solaroofing, 3DTIV, 3D Print Anything and Travelex), all selling to multicultural UAE audiences, and no marketing team yet.

I started on my own, set things up from scratch, and later built a small team.

(02)What I did

  • Ran a SWOT analysis for each business and built a strategy for multicultural UAE audiences. (3DTIV and Solaroofing have their own case studies.)
  • Built and led a multicultural marketing team, with members from France, Lebanon, Canada and Morocco.
  • Planned and ran social media on Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and X, with monthly KPI-based reporting.
  • Conceptualized and produced reels and short-form videos, from script to launch.
  • Launched Meta and Google Ads campaigns and optimized them regularly.
  • Led marketing for Expo events to raise brand presence.
  • Rebuilt the websites for SEO and managed cross-cultural, cross-functional projects, including presentations.

Sales of cabins and industrial 3D printers

Within a year, the Solaroofing team hit its target for reducing project downtime, cabin and 3D-printer sales doubled, and we launched a new business that is doing well. The websites now rank on the first page of search results.

(04)What I took away

There was no roadmap, only data I had to track down myself and a lot of pressure. You don't need everything to be perfect to start; you just need to start. Marketing to such a diverse audience taught me more about people than any course.

Fun fact

This was my first time living in a completely different country, with a new job and a six-day work week on top. A big shift, and a great learning experience.
