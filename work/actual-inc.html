Case study 01

ACTUAL Inc.

RoleMarketing Director (Sept 2025 – Mar 2026), then Global PR & Marketing Specialist
WhenSept 2025 – Sept 2026
PortfolioArchiX AI, WHERENESS, ART360, Sekaitoon, kitakita.ai
WhereKyoto, Japan

(01)The challenge

ACTUAL was a small Kyoto company with several ambitious products and a global audience in mind.

When I joined, the products were interesting, but the company was still figuring out how to explain them outside Japan. My job became a mixture of marketing strategy, positioning, partnerships, content and communications: basically, helping turn complicated technology into something people could understand.

(02)What I worked on

Browser-based 360° and 3D experiences

WHERENESS

Store, build and share 360° video, photos and 3D Gaussian Splatting scans as interactive tours in any browser. Used by museums, theaters, architects and researchers.

hello.whereness.io/ja AI rendering for architects and real-estate teams

ArchiX

Photoreal renders from sketches, CG or photos in about 30 seconds, plus AI staging, editing and walkthrough video. 3,000+ active users; launched in Japan, then globally.

archix.ai Digital archives of art exhibitions

ART360°

An online archive of art exhibitions recorded in 360°, so shows stay open to visitors anywhere after they close.

art360.place AI-assisted manga creation

Sekaitoon

Turns rough sketches into 4K manga backgrounds in seconds, with a consistent style from scene to scene.

sekaitoon.ai

(03)What I did

  • Led ArchiX AI's go-to-market, positioning and brand, and set the direction for how ACTUAL talked about its other AI products.
  • Reached out to potential partners directly instead of paying for acquisition. Around 30% of the companies I contacted replied.
  • Grew ArchiX's social media from 0 to 450 followers in its first week, built ACTUAL's Instagram and Medium from scratch, and ran email marketing and content across the website, social media and digital ads.
  • Worked closely with product, engineering and design to turn complex AI and 3D technology into plain messaging, press materials and launch communications.
  • Watched how people engaged, and kept adjusting what we said and where we said it.

(04)What happened

~30%
Response rate from organic partnership outreach
0 → 450
ArchiX social followers in the first week
5 countries
Where partnerships were established: Malaysia, Indonesia, Singapore, South Korea, Japan

ArchiX AI signed formal partnerships in Malaysia, Indonesia, Singapore, South Korea and Japan. One of them is T. R. Hamzah & Yeang, the Malaysian ecological-architecture firm, which found us through our organic marketing. From April 2026 my title was Global PR & Marketing Specialist, and I kept leading communications for the portfolio.

(05)What I took away

Fun fact

Japan showed me what deep commitment to work looks like, and I have real respect for it. It also taught me something about myself: I do my best, most creative work with a fully remote setup, clear expectations and room to rest. Knowing that isn't giving up. It's how I'm choosing where I'll grow next.
