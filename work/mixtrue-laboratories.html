Case study 08

Mixtrue Laboratories

RoleSocial Media Manager & Digital Marketing Consultant
When2020 – 2023
ChannelsTikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Shopee, Lazada
WherePhilippines

(01)The challenge

A local beauty and wellness manufacturer trying to stand out in a crowded skincare market. I took the role to go deeper into an industry I love.

(02)What I did

  • Managed the brand's TikTok, Instagram and Facebook, creating content that explained each product's value.
  • Planned digital promotions and ran user-generated content campaigns.
  • Worked with influencers on honest product reviews to build trust.
  • Managed the Shopee and Lazada stores and raised product ratings.
  • Helped launch a TikTok brand for the founder, because people connect with a person more than a brand.
  • Applied what I learned at L'Oréal about diversity, brand integrity and consumer-first innovation.

(03)What happened

0 → 130K+
Organic TikTok followers
1.4M
TikTok likes
4.8★
Rating on e-commerce platforms

The brand grew a lot during a period when shopping habits were changing quickly. We experimented with content, UGC, influencers and social commerce, and kept adjusting based on what people responded to.

(04)What I took away

Patience, and reading the market the way you read a room.

Fun fact

I tried every product in the range and even made my own custom soap for my skin.
