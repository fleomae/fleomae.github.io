Case study 10

Aeronamic HVAC

RoleMarketing Manager (working student)
WhenOct 2014 – Jun 2021
ChannelsFace-to-face promotion, social media, loyalty program
WherePhilippines

(01)The challenge

A small, new HVAC service company with a limited budget that needed local customers. I joined as a working student and stayed for the whole of my degree.

(02)What I did

  • Ran face-to-face promotions with local businesses, including a loyalty program flyer that rewarded repeat bookings.
  • Ran the company's social media campaigns.
  • Handled customer relations and bookings with a Japanese-Filipino supervisor, who taught me omotenashi (Japanese hospitality) in how we treated customers.
  • Kept the monthly and yearly tax records and other business administration.

(03)What happened

300%
More appointments within three months of the loyalty program
3×
Sales

Appointments grew quickly, and the loyalty program brought in customers who kept coming back.

(04)What I took away

Balancing work and university taught me to plan carefully and keep things simple. It's also where I learned the basics of business administration and customer care.

Fun fact

This job helped pay for my university fees, books and living costs.
