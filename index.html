(A little about me)

Marketer by trade, curious by nature.

I'm a marketer based in Kyoto, with 12 years of experience across quite a few different kinds of businesses.

I started working while I was still at university in the Philippines, then moved through agency work, consumer brands, e-commerce, B2B, sustainable energy, community projects and, most recently, AI products in Japan.

The industries have changed quite a lot. The part I enjoy has stayed pretty consistent: understanding who we're trying to reach, finding a clear way to talk to them, and figuring out what actually works. Mostly, I like helping good products find the people who need them.

Most of what I know about people I learned outside an office: on youth councils, in live-production booths, and underwater.

My approach

It's pretty simple, and it has worked in every industry I've been in.

  1. (01)

    Understand the audience.

    Before deciding what to say, understand who you're talking to.

  2. (02)

    Keep the message clear.

    Good marketing doesn't need to make things complicated.

  3. (03)

    Measure what works.

    Use the numbers, feedback and real-world response to decide what to do next.
